Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Rhodope Labs, Plovdiv, Bulgaria
In short: Kasovo has no server, no accounts and collects no data. Everything you enter — purchases, receipt photos, dates — stays on your iPhone.

1. Data the app handles

Kasovo stores only what you enter: product name, store, purchase and delivery dates, price, serial number, notes and receipt photos. This is saved locally in the app's protected storage on your device.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not send data to our or anyone else's servers.
  • We do not require sign-up, email or phone number.
  • We use no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • We do not share or sell data to third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Camera and Photos — only to capture or pick a receipt photo. Photos are copied into the app compressed, with location data removed (EXIF/GPS stripped).
  • Notifications — for reminders before a warranty or return window ends. Created and shown locally on the device.

4. Backup and export

You can export all your data as a ZIP file and restore it on another device. Where you send that file (AirDrop, iCloud Drive, email) is your choice and under your control. If you use iCloud backup for your iPhone, the app's data is included under Apple's policy.

5. In-app purchases

Kasovo Pro is unlocked through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. See Apple's privacy policy.

6. Deleting data

Deleting an item deletes its photos. Deleting the app deletes everything. There is nothing to request from us, because nothing is with us.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Material changes are noted in the App Store description.
